About Alisar Group

Built in Glasgow for the long term

A patient, own-capital property investment group — acquiring, improving and managing quality property across Scotland.

Our story

Prudence, patience and plain dealing.

Alisar Group Ltd was founded on a simple conviction: that the best property investments are made by people who use their own capital, think in decades, and treat every transaction as a relationship rather than a deal.

We don't manage other people's money. We don't chase volume or leverage. We acquire quality property in markets we understand, improve it thoughtfully, and hold it productively — building lasting value through careful stewardship rather than speculation.

Our name, Alisar, reflects our aspiration: to be a steady, trustworthy presence in Scottish property for years to come.

🎯

Our mission

To build a durable portfolio of quality Scottish property — acquired prudently, improved thoughtfully and managed to a high standard — generating reliable income and lasting capital value for the long term.

👁️

Our vision

To be recognised as one of Scotland's most trusted property investment groups — known for transparent dealings, quality stewardship and a genuinely long-term approach that benefits tenants, partners and communities alike.

Core values

The principles we're built on

Five values that predate our incorporation and guide every decision we make — from a first viewing to a tenancy renewal.

⚖️

Prudence

We protect capital first. Every acquisition must stand on its own merits, not on optimism or leverage.

❤️

Patience

We resist the pressure to act for the sake of acting. The right opportunity is worth waiting for.

👥

Fairness

We deal honestly with sellers, tenants and partners. Reputation is built slowly and lost quickly.

🎯

Discipline

Clear criteria, consistent process, no exceptions. Emotion doesn't drive our decisions.

👁️

Transparency

Plain English, honest figures, open communication. No small print, no surprises.
