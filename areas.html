Market profiles

Areas in focus

Each area has its own character, demand drivers and opportunity set. Here's a snapshot of the markets we cover.

Glasgow

City
📈 Strong

Scotland's largest city and our home market. From the West End's sandstone tenements to Merchant City apartments and the South Side's family neighbourhoods, Glasgow offers diverse property types, strong rental demand from students and professionals, and genuine long-term growth potential.

West End & HillheadMerchant CityShawlands & South SideDennistoun & East End

Lanarkshire

Region
📈 Growing

To the south-east of Glasgow, Lanarkshire offers affordable property with excellent commuter links. Towns like Rutherglen, Bellshill and Motherwell provide opportunities for value-add acquisition and refurbishment, with growing rental demand driven by Glasgow commuters.

RutherglenBellshillMotherwellEast Kilbride

Edinburgh

City
📈 Strong

Scotland's capital and one of the UK's strongest property markets. While typically higher-priced than Glasgow, Edinburgh's global appeal, limited supply and robust rental demand make it a market we monitor closely for selective acquisitions.

City CentreLeithMarchmontPortobello

Wider Scotland

Region
📈 Selective

Beyond the Central Belt, we selectively consider opportunities in areas with strong local economies, good transport links and proven rental demand — including Stirling, Dundee and the commuter belt surrounding Glasgow and Edinburgh.

StirlingDundeeFalkirkCommuter belt
