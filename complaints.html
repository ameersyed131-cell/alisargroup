Complaints Procedure

Last updated: October 2025

Alisar Group Ltd is committed to dealing with complaints fairly, promptly and transparently. This procedure explains how to raise a complaint and how we will handle it.

1. Our commitment

We take all complaints seriously and aim to resolve them as quickly as possible. We view complaints as an opportunity to learn and improve our service.

2. How to make a complaint

You can make a complaint by any of the following methods:

  • Email: hello@alisargroup.co.uk (mark the subject 'Complaint')
  • Phone: 0141 212 5000 during office hours
  • Post: Alisar Group Ltd, 40 Barony Drive, Ballieston, Glasgow, G69 6TN

3. What to include

To help us resolve your complaint quickly, please provide: your name and contact details, a clear description of the complaint, relevant dates and reference numbers, and the outcome you are seeking.

4. Our process

We follow a clear, staged process:

  • Stage 1 — Acknowledgement: We will acknowledge your complaint within 5 working days.
  • Stage 2 — Investigation: A senior member of the team will investigate and aim to provide a full response within 15 working days.
  • Stage 3 — Resolution: Where possible, we will propose a resolution. If we cannot resolve the matter, we will explain why and advise on further options.

5. If you remain dissatisfied

If you are not satisfied with our response, you may escalate the matter. Depending on the nature of the complaint, you may have the right to contact relevant regulatory bodies or the courts.

For tenancy-related complaints, you may also seek advice from your local authority's housing standards team or a citizens advice bureau.

6. Confidentiality

All complaints are handled in confidence. Information about your complaint will only be shared with those involved in investigating and resolving it.

This procedure is provided for informational purposes and does not constitute legal advice. We recommend reviewing it with your legal adviser before launch.
