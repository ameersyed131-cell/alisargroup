Cookie Policy

Last updated: October 2025

This policy explains how Alisar Group Ltd uses cookies and similar technologies on this website, and how you can control them.

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They help the site function, remember your preferences, and provide anonymised usage data to site owners.

2. Types of cookies we use

We use the following categories of cookies:

  • Essential cookies: required for the website to function (e.g., remembering your cookie consent choice). These cannot be disabled.
  • Analytics cookies: help us understand how visitors use the site so we can improve it. These are optional and only set if you accept them.
  • Preference cookies: remember choices you make (such as language or display preferences).

3. Third-party cookies

We do not sell your data to third parties. Where third-party services are used (such as map embeds or analytics), they may set their own cookies. We recommend reviewing their respective privacy policies.

4. Managing cookies

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. The cookie consent banner on this site allows you to accept or decline non-essential cookies. You can change your choice at any time by clearing your browser's cookie storage for this site.

Note that disabling essential cookies may affect the functionality of the website.

5. How long cookies stay

Session cookies are deleted when you close your browser. Persistent cookies remain on your device for a set period or until you delete them manually.

6. Contact

For any questions about our use of cookies, contact us at hello@alisargroup.co.uk.

This policy is provided for informational purposes and does not constitute legal advice. We recommend reviewing it with your legal adviser before launch.
