Who we are

A Glasgow property group built on prudence and patience.

Alisar Group Ltd was incorporated in Glasgow to acquire, improve and manage residential and commercial property across Scotland. We invest our own capital, hold for the long term, and deal plainly with everyone we work with — sellers, tenants, agents and partners alike.

Our approach is deliberately unhurried. We would rather make fewer, better acquisitions than chase volume. Every property we take on is chosen because it can be improved, well-managed and held productively for years to come.

Investment approach

From opportunity to asset, in four steps

Our process is straightforward and repeatable. It's designed to remove emotion, manage risk and ensure every acquisition earns its place in the portfolio.

🔍
1

Identify

We source opportunities through agents, auctions and private channels, filtering for location, condition and long-term potential.

📋
2

Evaluate

Every property is assessed against our acquisition criteria — price, yield, refurbishment scope, tenant demand and hold viability.

🔑
3

Acquire

We move quickly and transparently, using our own capital. No financing delays, no chain dependencies, no drawn-out negotiations.

📈
4

Hold and grow

We improve, let and manage the asset for the long term, compounding value through quality stewardship and considered upgrades.
