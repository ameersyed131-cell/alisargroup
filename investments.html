Investments

A disciplined approach to property investing

We invest our own capital in Scottish property — patiently, transparently and with clear criteria. Here's how we think about acquisitions, risk and long-term value.

Investment philosophy

Buy well, improve thoughtfully, hold patiently.

Our philosophy is simple: we'd rather own fewer, better properties acquired at the right price than chase volume. Every acquisition must work as a long-term hold — not just as a trade.

We invest our own capital, which means we answer only to ourselves. There are no external fund pressures, no quarterly redemption windows, no incentive to force activity. That freedom lets us be genuinely patient.

We focus on properties where we can add value — through refurbishment, better management, or simply time. We believe that quality compounds, and that well-maintained property in good locations will reward the patient owner.

Risk management

Protecting capital comes first

Property investment involves risk. We manage it through diversification, conservative financing, thorough due diligence and a refusal to overpay.

⚠️

Conservative leverage

We primarily use own capital. Where leverage is used, it's modest and structured for long-term stability.

📍

Geographic focus

We concentrate on markets we know — Glasgow and the Central Belt — rather than spreading thin across unfamiliar areas.

📋

Thorough due diligence

Every acquisition goes through legal, structural and financial checks before we commit. No shortcuts.

Anatomy of a deal

A sample acquisition, step by step

This is an illustrative example of how a typical Alisar acquisition unfolds. Names and figures are sample content for demonstration.

🔍
Step 1

Sourcing

A two-bedroom sandstone flat in a popular Glasgow neighbourhood comes to our attention through an agent contact. We review the listing, photos and area data.

📋
Step 2

Evaluation

We visit the property, assess condition and refurbishment scope, model rental income and costs, and benchmark against recent local comparables. The numbers work for a long-term hold.

🔑
Step 3

Acquisition

We make a fair, transparent offer at the guide price. Using our own capital, we complete quickly — no chain, no financing delays. The seller appreciates the clean process.

📈
Step 4

Improvement & letting

We refurbish the kitchen and bathroom, upgrade the EPC rating, and redecorate throughout. Within three weeks the flat is let to a referenced tenant at a fair market rent.

FAQ

Questions about our investment approach

Do you invest outside Scotland?▼

Our focus is Scotland — primarily Glasgow, Lanarkshire and Edinburgh. We invest in markets we can assess first-hand and manage effectively.

Do you use leverage or mortgages?▼

We primarily invest our own capital. Where leverage is used, it's conservative and structured to support long-term holding, not to amplify short-term returns.

What types of property do you avoid?▼

We avoid speculative off-plan purchases, highly leveraged structures, properties with unclear legal title, and anything where the numbers only work on optimistic assumptions.

Will you buy my property?▼

If your property meets our criteria and you're looking for a straightforward, transparent sale, we'd be glad to discuss it. Submit the form below and we'll be in touch.

Do you partner with other investors?▼

We're open to selective partnerships and joint ventures where there's genuine alignment of interest and a long-term horizon. Get in touch to discuss.

Selling a property or exploring a partnership?

Whether you have a property to sell, a site to develop, or are exploring a long-term investment partnership, we'd welcome a confidential conversation.
