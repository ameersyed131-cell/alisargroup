FAQ

Questions about selling to us

What types of property do you buy?▼

We buy residential and selected commercial property across Glasgow, Lanarkshire, Edinburgh and the Central Belt. Flats, terraces, semi-detached houses and small commercial units all considered.

Do you buy properties that need work?▼

Yes — properties needing refurbishment or modernisation are often a good fit for us. We assess the work required and factor it into our offer.

Are you a cash buyer?▼

We invest our own capital, which means we can complete quickly without mortgage approval delays. We're not a 'we buy any house' quick-sale company — we pay fair market value.

Is there any obligation?▼

None. Submitting your details and receiving an offer places you under no obligation whatsoever. The decision is always yours.

Selling a property or exploring a partnership?

Whether you have a property to sell, a site to develop, or are exploring a long-term investment partnership, we'd welcome a confidential conversation.
