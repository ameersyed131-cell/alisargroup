FAQ

Questions about partnering with us

What types of partnerships do you consider?▼

We're open to joint ventures on specific acquisitions, co-investment in portfolios, development partnerships and portfolio acquisition partnerships. We're flexible on structure but firm on alignment — we only partner with investors who share our long-term, transparent approach.

What's the minimum partnership commitment?▼

There's no fixed minimum — it depends on the opportunity and the structure. We've had conversations ranging from individual co-investments to multi-property portfolio partnerships. Get in touch and we'll discuss what works.

How are investment decisions made?▼

It depends on the structure. In joint ventures, decisions are typically shared. In co-investment structures, Alisar manages day-to-day decisions with transparent reporting to partners. Every partnership agreement sets out decision rights clearly upfront.

How long are partnership commitments?▼

Our horizon is long-term — typically 5 to 20+ years. We don't do short-term speculative partnerships. That said, partnership agreements can include defined exit points or review mechanisms if both parties agree.

What returns do you target?▼

We don't promise specific returns — property investment involves risk and returns depend on many factors. We focus on acquiring well, improving thoughtfully and holding patiently. Any return targets are discussed and agreed in the partnership agreement, not advertised.

Do you partner with individuals or only firms?▼

Both. We've had conversations with individual investors, family offices, property companies and institutional co-investors. What matters is alignment of philosophy and approach, not the size or type of the entity.

Whether you have a property to sell, a site to develop, or are exploring a long-term investment partnership, we'd welcome a confidential conversation.
