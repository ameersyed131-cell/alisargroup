Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2025

Alisar Group Ltd is committed to protecting your personal data. This policy explains what we collect, why we collect it, and your rights under UK GDPR.

1. Who we are

Alisar Group Ltd is a company registered in Scotland (company number SC867634) with its registered office at 40 Barony Drive, Ballieston, Glasgow, G69 6TN.

We are the data controller for the personal information we collect through this website and our business operations.

2. What data we collect

We may collect the following types of personal data:

  • Contact details: name, email address, phone number (when you submit an enquiry or newsletter signup)
  • Property details: information you provide about properties you wish to sell or enquire about
  • Usage data: anonymised analytics about how you use this website (if you accept analytics cookies)
  • Correspondence: records of communications you have with us

3. Why we collect it

We process your personal data for the following lawful purposes:

  • To respond to your enquiries and provide the services you request
  • To process property transactions and tenancy applications
  • To send you our newsletter (only if you have subscribed)
  • To comply with legal and regulatory obligations, including AML checks
  • To improve our website and services

4. Legal basis for processing

We rely on the following lawful bases under UK GDPR: consent (for newsletter and marketing), contract (for tenancy and service enquiries), legitimate interests (for business communications and improvement), and legal obligation (for compliance checks).

5. How long we keep your data

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, or as required by law. Enquiry data is typically retained for 24 months unless a transaction results. Tenancy-related data is retained for the duration of the tenancy plus six years for legal compliance.

6. Your rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the following rights:

  • The right to access your personal data
  • The right to rectify inaccurate data
  • The right to erase your data (the 'right to be forgotten')
  • The right to restrict or object to processing
  • The right to data portability
  • The right to withdraw consent at any time

7. Contact us

To exercise your rights or ask any question about your personal data, contact us at hello@alisargroup.co.uk or write to 40 Barony Drive, Ballieston, Glasgow, G69 6TN.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

This policy is provided for informational purposes and does not constitute legal advice. We recommend reviewing it with your legal adviser before launch.
