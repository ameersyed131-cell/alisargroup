Services

Everything we do, under one roof

From finding the right property to managing it for years afterwards — six services grounded in the same philosophy of disciplined acquisition, quality management and long-term holding.

Alisar Group is a full-cycle property investment business. We don't just buy property — we improve it, let it, manage it and maintain it. That vertical integration means we control quality at every stage, and our interests are always aligned with the long-term health of each asset.

FAQ

Common questions about our services

Do you manage properties for other landlords?▼

We currently manage our own portfolio. If you're a landlord looking for a management partner, we'd be happy to discuss — get in touch via our contact page.

Can you help me sell my property?▼

Yes — if your property meets our acquisition criteria, we may buy it directly. This means a fast, transparent sale with no chain. Submit an enquiry and we'll assess it.

Do you charge tenants any fees?▼

We comply fully with Scottish letting regulations, which prohibit most tenant fees. Tenants pay rent and a refundable deposit — no hidden charges.

How quickly can you complete a purchase?▼

Because we use our own capital, we can often complete faster than a typical buyer — typically within 2–4 weeks subject to legal checks.

Which service can we help with?

Whether you're selling a property, looking for a tenancy, or exploring a partnership — we'd be glad to discuss how we can help.
