Tenant FAQ

Deposits, repairs and your rights

How much is the deposit?▼

The deposit is typically one month's rent, held in an approved tenancy deposit scheme as required by Scottish law. It's fully refundable at the end of the tenancy, subject to the property being left in good condition.

How long is the tenancy?▼

Our tenancies are typically a minimum of six months, continuing on a rolling monthly basis after the initial period. We're happy to discuss longer-term arrangements.

How do I report a repair?▼

You can report repairs using the maintenance request form on this page, or by calling our office. Emergency repairs are prioritised. We aim to respond to all maintenance requests within 48 hours.

What are my rights as a tenant?▼

Scottish tenancy law provides strong protections for tenants, including the right to a safe, habitable home, protection from unfair eviction, and a clear complaints process. We respect and uphold all tenant rights.

Can I decorate or make changes?▼

Minor changes may be permitted with our prior written approval. Please contact us before making any alterations to the property.
